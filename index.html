ABOUT LUXE INTERIOR DESIGN BY MG

Luxe Interior Design by MG is an Orange County interior design studio serving Newport Beach and beyond. I offer full-service residential and commercial interior design, from renovations to furnishing and styling, with every project rooted in passion, artistry, and your individual lifestyle.

Throughout my career, I have completed a wide range of projects, from full renovations to complete furnishing and styling, always approaching each space with creativity, precision, and a deep appreciation for aesthetics. My education in both graphic and interior design allows me to approach every project with a refined understanding of color, composition, and proportion.

I believe that every home or business has its own story to tell, a story that deserves to be expressed as a work of art. With this philosophy, I approach each project as a unique creation, blending creativity, sophistication, and purpose.

Influenced by world travel, culture, fashion, and design, both classic and contemporary, I bring a refined yet adventurous sensibility to every space. The result is a thoughtfully curated environment that not only reflects my clients' personalities but also leaves a lasting impression.

OUR SERVICES

Full-Service Interior Design

Artistry in every space. Care in every detail.

Luxe Interior Design by MG offers full-service interior design for new construction, renovations, and furnishings, creating residential and commercial spaces that reflect your vision and the way you live. Serving Orange County, Newport Beach, and beyond, the studio brings thoughtful design and a personal approach to every project.

Ways to Work Together

New Construction

A considered interior vision for new homes and commercial spaces. From thoughtful layouts to coordinated materials and finishes, each design decision brings beauty, function, and your personal aesthetic into the space from the beginning.

Remodels & Renovations

Reimagine the spaces you already love. Interior design for renovations brings a fresh perspective to layouts, kitchens, bathrooms, and finishes, creating a cohesive result that reflects your lifestyle and the character of your property.

Furnishings & Styling

Furniture, lighting, textiles, artwork, and finishing touches come together in a space that feels distinctly yours. A thoughtful balance of color, texture, and proportion turns individual pieces into a complete, artful interior.

Thoughtfully Considered, From Concept to Completion

Space planning. Materials and finishes. Kitchen and bathroom design. Furniture and lighting selection. Art, accessories, and final styling. Each element is selected with the whole space in mind, bringing your vision into a cohesive design.
